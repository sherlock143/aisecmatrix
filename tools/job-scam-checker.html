Fake Job Offer Checker

Paste a job offer, recruiter message, or interview invite. This checks it against patterns common to fake job scams — advance "training fee" requests, work-from-home schemes, and WhatsApp-only recruiters. Runs entirely in your browser.

pattern-match.local — offline
This is pattern-matching, not proof — some real offers can trip a flag, and some scams have none. Before accepting any job, verify the company independently (its own website, LinkedIn, calling their listed office number) and never pay money to get hired, for training, a "starter kit," or equipment.
