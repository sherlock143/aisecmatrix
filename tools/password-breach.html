Password Breach Checker

Checks your password against a database of over 900 million passwords exposed in real breaches — powered by Have I Been Pwned's Pwned Passwords API. Your password is never sent in full: only the first 5 characters of its hash leave your browser. This is called k-anonymity.

pwnedpasswords.com — k-anonymity lookup
How this stays private: your password is hashed (SHA-1) inside your browser. Only the first 5 characters of that hash are sent to the API. The API returns every breached password that shares that 5-character prefix — typically hundreds of them — and the match is confirmed locally on your device. Your actual password never crosses the network.
