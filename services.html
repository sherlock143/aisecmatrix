How an engagement works

①

Scope & kickoff

We define target systems, rules of engagement, and timeline together before any testing begins.

②

Manual + tooled testing

Findings come from hands-on testing, not just an automated scanner output with a logo slapped on it.

③

Findings report

Each finding includes severity, reproduction steps, business impact, and a remediation path — mapped to relevant standards (OWASP, PCI DSS where applicable).

④

Retest

Once fixes are in place, we verify them — a report with no retest is an unfinished engagement.
